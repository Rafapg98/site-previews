Deep Cleaning vs. Regular Cleaning: What's the Difference — and Which One Does Your Home Need?

By SL Empire House Cleaning Services · November 26, 2026
Illustrative photo

It's the most common question we hear: "Do I need a deep cleaning, or just a regular one?" The two services sound similar, but they do different jobs. Here's how to tell them apart — and how to know which one your home needs right now.

What a regular cleaning covers

A regular cleaning is maintenance. It assumes your home is already in decent shape and keeps it there: kitchen counters and sink sanitized, bathrooms cleaned and disinfected, surfaces dusted, mirrors polished, and floors vacuumed and mopped throughout. It's the visit that stops the slow build-up of everyday life — cooking splashes, bathroom moisture, dust, pet hair — before it becomes a project.

Regular cleanings are booked on a rhythm: weekly, biweekly or monthly. The per-visit price is lower because the home never gets far behind.

What a deep cleaning adds

A deep cleaning is a reset. It includes everything in a regular cleaning, plus the areas that build up quietly over months:

  • Baseboards, door frames, vents and ceiling fans
  • Detailed scrubbing of showers, tubs and tile grout lines
  • Inside the oven and refrigerator (on request)
  • Cabinet fronts degreased, especially around the stove
  • Window sills, tracks and interior glass
  • Behind and under movable furniture and appliances

It takes longer and costs more than a regular visit — because it's doing months of catch-up in a single appointment.

Which one does your home need?

Choose a deep cleaning if any of these sound familiar: it's your first professional cleaning, it has been more than a month or two since the last thorough clean, you're hosting guests or listing the home, or the bathrooms and kitchen have visible build-up. Choose a regular cleaning if the home is already in good shape and you want to keep it that way without spending your weekends on it.

That's why most of our recurring clients follow the same pattern: one deep clean to reset the home, then a regular rhythm to hold it. From there, a lighter deep clean once or twice a year keeps the hidden areas honest.

The honest bottom line

If you're not sure, send us a few photos of your kitchen and bathrooms when you ask for your free estimate. We'll tell you plainly which one your home needs — including if the answer is "regular is enough."

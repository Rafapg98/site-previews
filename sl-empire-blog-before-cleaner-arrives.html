Before Your Cleaner Arrives: 9 Small Things That Make a Big Difference

By SL Empire House Cleaning Services · November 26, 2026
Illustrative photo

A cleaning visit goes further when a little prep happens first — not because you should clean before the cleaner (please don't), but because ten small minutes from you can buy thirty extra minutes of actual cleaning. Here's what our happiest clients do.

The ten-minute list

  • Clear the surfaces you'll want cleaned. Mail off the kitchen counter, toys off the floor, toiletries gathered in the bathroom. We clean surfaces — the less that's on them, the more of them we can reach.
  • Put away valuables and fragile items. Jewelry, heirlooms, that vase you love. It protects your things and lets us clean without tiptoeing.
  • Run the dishwasher or clear the sink. A sink we can access is a sink we can sanitize properly.
  • Secure the pets. We love them — but a closed room or the backyard keeps them safe and lets us move fast with doors open.
  • Point out your priorities. Guests coming? Focus the bathrooms. Allergies? Extra dusting. A quick text before the visit aims our time where it matters most to you.
  • Mention problem areas. The shower that's losing the fight with hard water, the oven after a spill — tell us, and we'll bring the right approach.
  • Decide on linens. Leave fresh sheets out if you'd like beds changed; it's a small thing that makes coming home feel like a hotel.
  • Sort out access. Key, code, garage — whatever you've arranged, a quick double-check avoids delays.
  • Leave feedback from last time. Loved something? Missed something? Tell us. The checklist improves with every note, and your home gets more "yours" each visit.

What you don't need to do

Don't pre-clean. Don't scrub the bathroom "so it doesn't look bad." Don't apologize for the state of anything — a lived-in home is the job, and we've seen it all. Your only homework is the list above; the rest is ours.

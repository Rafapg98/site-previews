Move-Out Cleaning Checklist: What Jacksonville Landlords and Buyers Actually Check

By SL Empire House Cleaning Services · November 26, 2026
Illustrative photo

When you hand back the keys, someone walks through your old home with a critical eye — and in rental homes, that walk-through decides how much of your deposit comes back. After years of move-out cleans across the Jacksonville area, here is what landlords, property managers and buyers actually check, room by room.

The kitchen decides first impressions

  • Inside the oven and refrigerator — the two most-checked spots in any walk-through
  • Stovetop and range hood degreased
  • Cabinets and drawers emptied, wiped inside and out
  • Sink polished, faucet descaled, counters sanitized
  • Floors swept and mopped, including corners and under the fridge if movable

Bathrooms: where deposits go to die

  • Shower and tub scrubbed — soap scum and mildew are the top complaints
  • Toilet cleaned inside, outside and behind
  • Mirrors, fixtures and counters polished
  • Cabinets and drawers wiped out
  • Exhaust fan cover dusted

Everywhere else

  • Baseboards, door frames and light switches wiped
  • Ceiling fans and vents dusted
  • Interior windows and tracks cleaned
  • Walls spot-checked for marks and scuffs
  • All floors vacuumed and mopped; closets emptied and swept
  • Every last item, nail and piece of trash removed — "broom clean" is the floor, not the goal

Timing matters as much as the clean

Schedule the cleaning after everything is out and before the walk-through — ideally the day before you hand back the keys. Cleaning around boxes and furniture wastes money and misses exactly the corners inspectors check. If you're a landlord or realtor between tenants, the same checklist applies: it's what makes a home photograph and show like it's ready.

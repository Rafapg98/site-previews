Weekly, Biweekly or Monthly House Cleaning: How to Choose the Right Rhythm for Your Home

By SL Empire House Cleaning Services · November 26, 2026
Illustrative photo

Once you've decided to hand the cleaning over, the next question is rhythm: how often should the cleaner come? There's no single right answer — but there is a right answer for your household. Here's the framework we use with our own clients in the Jacksonville area.

Choose weekly if…

  • Your home has kids, pets, or both — and the floors prove it daily
  • Someone cooks most nights (kitchens build up fast)
  • You host often, or simply want the house always ready
  • Allergies make dust control a health issue, not a preference

Weekly clients get the lowest per-visit price, because each visit is pure maintenance — nothing ever gets a chance to build up.

Choose biweekly if…

  • Your household is busy but not chaotic
  • You can keep up with the small stuff (dishes, quick tidying) between visits
  • You want bathrooms and kitchen professionally reset twice a month

Biweekly is the rhythm most of our clients choose, and it's easy to see why: it's the sweet spot between cost and control. The home never gets more than two weeks away from a full professional reset.

Choose monthly if…

  • You live alone or as a couple in a smaller home
  • You genuinely keep up with cleaning between visits
  • You mainly want help with the heavy jobs — bathrooms, floors, kitchen detail

A monthly visit leans deeper than a standard regular clean, because four weeks of life needs a bit more undoing. It's a good fit for tidy households — and an easy plan to upgrade later.

Three rules of thumb

Pets shorten the rhythm. Fur and tracked-in dirt roughly double how fast a home feels dirty. More people, more often. Every additional person adds bathroom and kitchen load. Start tighter, then relax. Many clients start biweekly, and once the home is under control, some step down to monthly — while others love the weekly feeling too much to give it up.
